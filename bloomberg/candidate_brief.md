# Workshop Signups: Candidate Brief (Round 2 style)

You've joined the team that owns this module. Read `README.md` for the
product behaviour and `TICKETS.md` for four customer-reported issues.

- The tickets are short and vague, as they would be in real life.
- There is one smoke test. Write your own failing tests before fixing anything.
- Keep public function signatures unchanged.
- You may use an AI assistant. You are expected to decide the approach and
  to verify everything it suggests.

Time: 45 minutes. At the end, be ready to explain your fixes, how you
validated them, remaining risks, and what would make this production ready.
