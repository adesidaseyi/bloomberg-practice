# lattice - Limit Order Book

`OrderBook` is a single-symbol limit order book with price-time priority. The
service is in-memory and single-process.

## Domain model

An `Order` has:

- `order_id` - generated by the service (`O1`, `O2`, ...)
- `side` - `buy` or `sell`
- `price_cents` - the limit price, a positive integer number of cents
- `qty` - the original quantity, a positive integer number of shares
- `remaining` - the quantity not yet executed
- `status` - one of `OPEN`, `PARTIAL`, `FILLED`, `CANCELLED`
- `seq` - the arrival sequence used for time priority

A `Trade` has:

- `trade_id` - generated by the service (`T1`, `T2`, ...)
- `price_cents` - the execution price
- `qty` - the executed quantity
- `buy_order_id` and `sell_order_id` - the two orders that met

The book holds resting bids and asks keyed by order id. The best bid is the
highest resting bid price; the best ask is the lowest resting ask price.

## Matching rules

- Price priority: a buy matches the lowest ask first; a sell matches the highest
  bid first.
- Time priority: within one price level, the oldest order (smallest `seq`)
  matches first.
- A marketable order sweeps successive price levels while it still crosses the
  opposite side: a buy crosses while `ask <= limit`; a sell crosses while
  `bid >= limit`.
- Each trade prints at the resting order's price, not at the incoming order's
  limit.
- A partial execution reduces both orders' `remaining`. A resting order at zero
  remaining leaves the book.
- Any unfilled remainder of the incoming order rests at its limit price.
- Whenever both sides of the book are non-empty, `best_bid < best_ask`.

## Lifecycle

| From | Event | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| new | no execution, rests | `OPEN` |
| new, `OPEN`, or `PARTIAL` | partial execution | `PARTIAL` |
| new, `OPEN`, or `PARTIAL` | full execution | `FILLED` |
| `OPEN` or `PARTIAL` | cancel | `CANCELLED` |
